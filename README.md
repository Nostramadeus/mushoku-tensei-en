# Mushoku Tensei — Volume 1

An **unofficial, AI-assisted English fan translation**, published by Nostramadeus.

**Quality disclaimer:** I do not vouch for the overall quality of this
translation. This is a fun AI-assisted experiment. I am not claiming it is
the best translation, or one of the best.

I wrote translation rules, audited dozens of passages against the Japanese,
compared them with the official English, and refined the rules from those
checks. I found errors in the official translation, but those findings do not
establish that this output is consistently accurate or superior.

I am not committing to polish all of the output. Mistranslations, awkward
phrasing, and inconsistent character voices may remain.

**Mature content:** sexual themes, violence, and disturbing material.

This reader contains Volume 1 only. Font size, theme, and reading progress are
remembered in your browser when local storage is available. No external fonts,
analytics, or tracking services are used.

Japanese source and official English reference books are excluded. Private
translation notes, comparisons, and review materials are also excluded.
Illustrations retain their original visible artwork text; English caption
translations appear directly below them where applicable.
